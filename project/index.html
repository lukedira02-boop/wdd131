Bird Watching Basics

Look for Habitat

Different birds prefer different environments. Check trees, grassland, wetlands, gardens, and water edges.

Listen for Calls

Bird calls can help you locate birds even when they are hidden among leaves or branches.

Watch the Weather

Comfortable conditions and good visibility can make an outdoor observation session more productive.

Responsible Exploration

Outdoor Safety Tips

  • Carry drinking water and protect yourself from the sun.
  • Tell someone where you are going when exploring unfamiliar areas.
  • Keep a respectful distance from wildlife and nesting areas.
  • Follow local park, trail, and conservation rules.
